<div class="content-page" markdown="0">

<p class="lead">I am a PhD candidate in economics at the <a href="https://www.parisschoolofeconomics.eu/en/persons/briam-e-guerrero-batista/" target="_blank">Paris School of Economics (PSE)</a> and a France Excellence Eiffel Doctoral Scholar. I work on labor and development economics, studying how skills shape occupational choice, informality, and labor-market segmentation in developing countries. My thesis committee consists of David N. Margolis (supervisor) and Karen Macours.</p>

<p class="lead">I teach economics at Sciences Po and at the Instituto Tecnológico de Santo Domingo (INTEC) in the Dominican Republic, where I also teach graduate courses in stochastic processes and mathematical finance for the MSc in Applied Mathematics. Before the PhD, I spent nearly seven years as an economist at the Central Bank of the Dominican Republic.</p>

<p class="lead">I hold a two-year Research Master's in Economics (APE) from the Paris School of Economics (PSE), and an MSc in Applied Mathematics and a BSc in Economics from the Instituto Tecnológico de Santo Domingo (INTEC).</p>
